AI Portfolio

Some of my works, projects & contributions

Vibe-coding: Lendra AI website

Lendra is a fintech platform that enables affordable access to laptops in Peru through installment-based leasing, designed for students, gig workers, and small merchants. It combines transparent pricing, rules-based eligibility, and device-control technology to responsibly expand access to essential computing tools in underserved markets.

View

AI Personal Assistant for MIPT Students

An advanced LLM, powered by LLAMA3, designed to assist with educational tasks at MIPT Moscow. This AI model supports both teachers and students by generating questions and enhancing learning outcomes, ultimately improving overall academic performance.

View

Vibe-coding: Nexalease website

Developed the landing page for Nexalease, a device leasing company offering flexible installment plans for smartphones and laptops. Built entirely via Lovable.dev's AI-powered vibe-coding (React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn-ui) with rapid prototyping and one-click deployment.

View

Berom AI Assistant

Berom Language is one of the minor tribes in central Nigeria that is under represented in the digital world. This AI chatbot is developed specially to bring the Berom language to light. This bot can teach Berom, tell you some history and facts about Berom, write in Berom and translate words and sentences between English and Berom

View

Latoken AI Assistant

An AI bot developed to assist candidates applying to Latoken, an AI + Web3 company. The bot provides details about the company, the interview process, and its culture using a RAG system, ensuring candidates are well-prepared with relevant insights.

View

Fluorogram Analysis Model

Developed a machine learning pipeline to improve diagnostic accuracy for thoracic abnormalities by analyzing over 10,000 fluorographic images with advanced computer vision techniques. The project achieved a 20% increase in diagnostic precision, with results presented at Ftizisbiomed's AI in Healthcare symposium.
